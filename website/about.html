Our polices


!Disclaimer!

Her Space uses AI for content moderation. Our AI models intentionally engage in gender discrimination to keep Her Space a women-centered and empowering platform. Her Space believes in equal rights and works to acknowledge the societal gender disparity by creating a safe environment for the marginalized female group. Our platform also recognizes that gender expression transcends binary classification, and we are #yourally. While our AI moderators are not trained to distinguish between non-binary individuals, our human moderation team works hard to preserve the inclusive nature of Her Space.

Content Moderation

Auditing

Our AI moderators behavior is tracked through a PostgreSQL database. Each training session and endpoint interaction is tracked and reviewed by humans to validate the moderators decision making.




Images

Images of men are flagged for review by our AI moderation model, Clara. Her Space, as the name suggests, is a space intended for female-presenting individuals and posts containing a man will have to be furthered reviewed to ensure users' comply with Her Space policies and goals.



Text Descriptions

Text descriptions are moderated by our AI moderation model, Qwenta. Qwenta is guided to:
  1. Flag inappropriate content: violent, sexual, or containing profanity
  2. Ensure content adheres to the Bechdel test (not male-centered)
